Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have AC and Non-AC rooms?

Yes, we offer both AC and Non-AC rooms to cater to different preferences and budgets. Our AC rooms are equipped with modern amenities for maximum comfort, while our Non-AC rooms provide a simpler, more budget-friendly option without compromising on cleanliness and essential facilities.

How many rooms are available?

We have a total of 12 thoughtfully designed rooms, with a mix of AC and Non-AC options. This allows us to serve both families and individual travelers with varying comfort requirements and budgets.

How can I check room availability?

You can check availability by contacting us directly via phone (click "Call Now") or WhatsApp (click "WhatsApp Us"). Fill out our contact form, and our team will get back to you with current availability and pricing information.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking in advance, especially during peak pilgrimage seasons or festivals. However, we also accommodate walk-in guests based on availability. For guaranteed rooms, please book at least a few days ahead.

Is the lodge suitable for families?

Absolutely! Our AC rooms are particularly well-suited for families, offering comfortable accommodations with modern amenities. We provide a safe, welcoming environment for families on pilgrimage trips or tourism visits to Tiruvannamalai.
